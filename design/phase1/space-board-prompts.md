# Space direction board prompts

Appearance targets only; these boards do not establish source geometry, rigging or shipped animation. Final local images: `img/space-carrier-a.png` and `img/space-relay-b.png`.

## A

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: BREACH mobile game design choice board, landscape 1536 by 1024.
Primary request: Show option A, a premium playful glossy 3D space brick-breaker chapter called "CARRIER BREAKOUT". Clear story purpose: free the little Clank worker robots from a hijacked mothership signal by breaking its relay armour and disabling its cannons. This is a new design proposal, not a finished game screenshot.
Composition: Left 45 percent is one large straight-on portrait phone playfield, no photoreal phone frame. Dark navy space, sparse dim stars and tiny distant violet planet. Top is a broad villain carrier with a visible recessed hangar and two chunky cannon pods, surrounded by small cyan/pink robot-brick rows. Three little fighter craft physically exit the open hangar in a descending arc. Cyan paddle and one white ball near lower playfield. A compact round face-only pilot orb in lower LEFT control deck; keep central thumb drag lane clear. HUD small and calm, no excessive meters. Right 55 percent is one clean character lineup and two horizontal sequences of three rendered motion key poses.
Dedicated cast: NIX is a compact small alien pilot in a cobalt flight suit with a continuous soft lilac face, small gill fins either side of head, short arms, stubby feet, large expressive eyelids/brows and mouth. Neutral, determined, startled face studies must be distinct. No human hair and no knight. TICK is a crab-shaped maintenance robot with six small mechanical legs, two asymmetrical practical tools and a broad optic panel, visibly different anatomy. CLANK workers have round toy heads and tiny limbs. Carrier villain is a magenta mechanical eye embedded in ship, not another humanoid.
Right sequences: hangar shut -> doors split and fighters nose out -> squadron banks toward playfield; a side cannon aim lamp -> short recoiling barrel discharge -> tangible blue deflector plates intercept impact. Use physical animation poses, no long magical light trail.
Style: polished toy-scale enamel, satin plastic and brushed metal, beveled silhouettes, coherent geometry, soft warm upper-left key light, cool fill and faint pink bounce. Rounded playful title typography, short legible labels only: "A  CARRIER BREAKOUT", "FREE THE CLANKS", "NIX", "TICK", "HANGAR LAUNCH", "DEFLECTOR". Plenty of dark negative space, restrained cyan/magenta/violet accents and almost no yellow. Not generic flat vector illustration, not a crowded app dashboard. All faces large enough to read; all figures/ships complete without cropping. No princess or snake.

## B

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: BREACH mobile game design choice board, landscape 1536 by 1024.
Primary request: Show option B, a premium playful glossy 3D space brick-breaker chapter called "RELAY RAIDERS". Distinct objective: a small alien salvage crew breaks a moving orbital jammer's locks, rescues trapped research drones and keeps the escape gate open. This is a new design proposal, not a finished game screenshot.
Composition: Left 45 percent is one large straight-on portrait phone playfield, no photoreal phone frame. Calm very dark navy space with dim stars, a distant blue crescent. At top a horseshoe orbital machine around a violet gate, two rotating short cannon pods, and four cargo pods in shaped rows of rounded cyan/violet armour bricks. A tiny friendly escape skiff waits by the gate. One cannon visibly warns with three segmented lit marks; another has a short tangible recoil pose. Cyan paddle and white bouncing ball at bottom. Lower-left face-only hero orb and a clean unoccupied central drag lane. No busy UI.
Right 55 percent shows dedicated cast and two three-keyframe mechanical action sequences. VESPER is a low four-legged alien star cat, dark lavender with broad continuous pale face, large amber eyelids, four weight-bearing paws and a long expressive steering tail whose tip has a soft fin; attentive, sly and startled face studies. No humanoid hands or arms, no upright human biped stance. LATCH is a small chubby alien lockfish, teal oval body with side fins, forked tail and continuous expressive jaw/gill mass; swimming, braked angry face and joyful relief. No humanoid arms, no manta wings, no shared body template. Vesper rides INSIDE a small transparent cockpit skiff during space gameplay, Latch lives in a round floating water field near the locks. Both must have purposeful roles.
Sequences: lockfish tail-driven swim -> jaws clamp an exposed metal latch -> gate iris physically opens; cannon sweeps a marked lane -> barrel recoils with short bolt -> captured drone exits opened pod toward skiff. Clear anticipation/contact/recovery poses.
Style: premium rendered glossy sculpted toys, bevels, clean body continuity, warm top-left key and cool fill with faint pink bounce. Restrained cyan/lavender/magenta; very little yellow. Rounded playful title and short legible labels only: "B  RELAY RAIDERS", "OPEN THE ESCAPE GATE", "VESPER", "LATCH", "LOCK AND RELEASE", "CANNON SWEEP". Dark negative space, clear hierarchy. Complete characters/ships, faces large enough to read. No princess, no knight and no snake; no generic long-arm creatures, no long magic light trails, no flat vector art.
