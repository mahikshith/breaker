## Refinement and limits

Both final studies retain the approved glossy machinery/brick families, subdued blackish planets, full-screen starfield, low control reserve and sparse HUD. Relay removes the upper pilot, gives the ship burst short impact-limited bolts instead of through-metal beams, and restores four empty escort stations. Orbital uses neutral life orbs, a neutraliser glyph, intact hull lip and exterior target. Earlier v1/v2 variants are retained as source history.

The Relay board's cargo size and machinery travel are illustrative, not scale-certified. The design contract requires constant32u cargo, a42u physically opened corridor and seven real breakable blockers; the sampled alpha blockout passes but continuous sweeps/new moving rails and barrel poses remain unproved. Do not copy the board's approximate sprite sizing into collision or remove metal invisibly. The Circuit craft needs a real upfield combat/morph family; its illustration is not a shipped pose.

The Orbital original bay has an opaque lower lip: existing shutter opening alone never creates an exterior ball route. Both proposals deliberately require a new visible external assembly. Actual ordinary/TITAN clearance, warning windows and machinery states must be proved before final asset admission. B's curved fields additionally need identical prediction/live integration and bounded influence.
