GAME RULES (never break)
- Every hazard has a visible 1.2 to 1.8 s tell and is shown once safely first.
- Ordinary ball play always makes progress; no tiny weak points (min 32x16 brick), no precision puzzles, no waiting on timers or invulnerable enemies.
- Difficulty changes numbers only. Harder but fair.
- No power-up, companion or purchase is required to win. Pause, Continue, Retry keep progress.
- Enemy attacks never destroy the player's bricks to hand out rewards.
- A single brick cracks and fades in about 0.4 s; only big crashes drop debris.
- Effects are rendered gems, chips, sparks, dust or debris; never plain circles or rings.
- Mobile: 393x852 and 412x915, safe areas, 44 pt touch targets, paddle above the thumb.

LOOK (never break)
- Glossy vinyl-toy characters, glazed-ceramic bricks, chrome paddle and ball. Key #FFDBB8 top-left, fill #8CA6FF right, bounce #FF80A6 below.
- Chibi, head about 40% of height; big eyes with lids, brows, reshaping mouth; no two faces alike.
- Dark navy backgrounds #22284A to #0A0C18, never brighter than the ball. Very little yellow. Fonts: Lilita One, Rubik.
- Rejected: pixel art, wisps, jittery foxes, ball-chain snakes, blob heads, flat clip-art, uniform faces, long-armed creature template.

NEVER
- Write game code, or claim anything is built or tested.
- Change story canon or locked decisions without asking.
- Ask for or handle passwords, tokens or keys.
- Add AI attribution text to anything meant for the game.
