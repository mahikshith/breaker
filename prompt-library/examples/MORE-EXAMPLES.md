## How to use them in Copilot

- Attach the one that matches the job (board, icon, prop, or "what it looks like in the game") together with 1 or 2 more from `../references.md`.
- Add this sentence to the prompt: "Match the attached images for materials, lighting, finish and scale. Do not copy their layout or their text."
- The in-game screenshot is the final test: if your result would not look right on that screen, it is off-style.
