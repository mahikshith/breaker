# Example pictures

Attach these to your chat as style references. All are downscaled to 1024 px wide or less and have no metadata. They show the look we want; they are not for copying exactly.

| File | What it shows | Attach it to |
|---|---|---|
| `gatehouse-first-pass.png` | Six phone screens of Gatehouse Siege (floors 1 to 3): dark gothic hall, glazed red/cream/sage bricks, counterweights, winch gate, Korr duel, HUD, low chrome paddle, Kit face orb. The target for level boards. | 30 Gatehouse Siege (all floors), 30 template, 20 section 4 (castle props) |
| `lumi-faces.png` | Kit and Lumi: six expressions each at 288 px, plus their 56 px face orbs and the chrome and pink-gold paddles. The standard for faces. | 10 Kit, Lumi, face orbs, expression sheets; 30 Lumi Breaks Out, Serpent Rivals |
| `kit-sprite-and-face.png` | Kit's full sprite at game size (a 42-unit-tall knight in a 168 px cell), then his idle, worried and cheer faces. Shows how small the character is in play. | 10 Kit (all), any new hero |
| `power-icons.png` | The nine power icons for Kit and Lumi: physical objects, not pictograms. | 20 section 7 (power-up icons) and section 8 (capsules) |
| `serpent-window.png` | The layered tower window: back recess, front lip, the serpent between them, native size. | 20 section 5 (window kit), 30 Lumi Breaks Out and Serpent Rivals, 10 Gnash |
| `snake-emerald-heads.png` | Eight of Gnash's 13 emerald head expressions (idle, hunt, hiss, strike, chew, ouch, dizzy, grin). Angular head, pink brows, never round. | 10 Gnash, 30 Lumi Breaks Out and Serpent Rivals |
| `korr-boar.png` | Korr, the armoured four-legged boar, idle pose. Glazed ruby and plum plates, ivory tusks, amber eyes. The bar for a new creature. | 10 Korr, Ember, Zephyr (for the armour look), 30 Gatehouse floors 2 and 3 |
| `nerra-crab.png` | Nerra, the hermit-crab queen with a fortress on her shell and two different claws. Shows a non-humanoid anatomy. | 10 Nerra, any new creature (anatomy card) |
| `vault-bricks.png` | Contact sheet of the Vault brick atlas: five colours, 1- and 2-cell bricks, three damage states, gold gem, granite, capsules, balls and paddle pieces. | 20 sections 1 to 3 and 8, 10 Grumble, 30 Dragon's Hoard |
| `vault-breach-screen.png` | A real in-game phone screenshot (393 x 852): dense glazed bricks, debris from a big crash, BREACH text, low paddle. | 20 section 6 (FX), 30 Dragon's Hoard |

Notes:

- `korr-boar.png`, `nerra-crab.png` and the sheet backgrounds are shown on dark navy only for viewing; the real game sprites are transparent.
- `kit-sprite-and-face.png`, `snake-emerald-heads.png`, `starflight-ships.png` and `vault-bricks.png` are contact sheets made from the real game files.
- Attach at most 3 to 4 pictures per prompt; more makes the tool lose the plot. Put the character reference first.
