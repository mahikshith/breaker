# 14 - Effects (breakage, sparks, shockwaves, power effects)

Purpose: generate effect art as **sprite-sheet friendly pieces**: isolated, centred, on transparency, in grids of equal cells, or as a left-to-right sequence of key stages. The game adds motion (translation, rotation, scale, fade, recoil) in code; the sheets supply the shapes. Use with `00-style-block.md` and references 18, 3 and 21 from `references.md`.

## The rules that matter most

1. **Physical, not magical.** Pieces of stone, metal, glass and sparks that obviously have mass. The owner rejected long magical light trails, perfect neon circles, particle halos and wisps. Show physical cause and effect.
2. **Single bricks vanish quietly.** A single broken brick cracks and fades in about 0.4 s. Do not make a spectacle for one brick. Only big structural crashes (drawbridge, tower, gate) get large chunks and a bigger burst.
3. **Debris is large clean chunks**, not dust or confetti. Three to five unequal chips per set. (The existing `chip_a`, `chip_b`, `chip_c` are the model.)
4. **Every hit gets particles, a small shake and a sound; big moments add hit-stop and big text.** The effect art must therefore read in 2 to 4 frames of attention. Brightest and largest at stage 2 or 3, then clearing.
5. **Bounded glow.** Controlled emission, no screen-filling bloom, nothing that hides the white ball. The white ball must stay findable at the peak of every effect.
6. **Enemy attacks never destroy the player's bricks to hand out free resources.** Do not draw hostile effects that eat bricks, unless that is the declared mechanic of the level (the old Vault serpent).
7. **Readable at 24 to 64 pixels**, like the Starflight weapon family. Strong silhouette, bright dense core, short turbulent tail.
8. **Transparent real alpha, no baked-in background, no ground, no ship or character in the frame.** Keep 10 percent empty padding inside each cell; effects must not touch the cell edge (the soft glow included).

## Sizes

| Piece | Size |
|---|---|
| Chips and sparks | each cell 512 x 512 in a 2048 x 2048 sheet; used in the game at about 4 to 16 units |
| Impact or explosion stage cell | 512 x 512, used at about 24 to 64 units |
| Sequence sheet | 8 stages in a 4 x 2 grid, each cell 512 x 512, sheet 2048 x 1024 |
| Pivot | The centre of every cell is the effect origin (impact point), for every stage |

## The families

| Family | Pieces | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Brick break** | 3 unequal stone or ceramic chips; 1 small glaze flake; a hairline crack overlay | No dust cloud. Tint comes from the brick colour in code, so draw chips neutral grey-brown plus a version in each brick colour if asked |
| **Structural crash** | 4 to 6 large chunks (wood plank, banded iron, stone slab, brass cap, chain link) | Only for big collapses. Chunks must look heavy |
| **Sparks** | Metal-on-metal ricochet spark star (4 stages); a small ember fleck set | Short hot sparks, bright white-gold cores fading to coral. Small |
| **Shockwave** | A ground-hugging dust and pressure ripple with a few flying fragments, in 6 to 8 stages | Irregular and physical. Not a perfect circle |
| **Power effects** | Ram Ball impact crash, Lullaby note wave, Brick Ward shield flare, Starlight Notes pickups drifting, Heat ember trail | Each with a clear physical cause (a ball hits a brick; a song wave leaves a note; a ward plate shines at its contact point) |
| **Cooldown and ready cues** | A ring on the paddle lighting 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 | Tiny, bright, readable at 9 units |

## Output spec

| Output | Spec |
|---|---|
| Sequence sheet | 2048 x 1024 PNG, transparent, 4 x 2 cells of 512 x 512, origin at each cell centre, same scale across stages |
| Piece sheet | 2048 x 2048 PNG, transparent, equal cells, one piece per cell |
| Tint variants | Same layout and same cell order per colour, so one script can swap them |
| Hand-back note | Effect name, cause, stage order, the chroma colour used if transparency failed, and how large it should be in game |

Filing: `art/<family>/source/` (pattern `art/starflight/weapons-source.png` plus `extract_weapons.py`, which gives each cell an 8-pixel transparent border and strips metadata), then the shared FX kit (`art/fx_kit/fx_kit.py`) for anything that must match the bricks exactly. The runtime adds translation, recoil, rotation and scaling to static sprites; these sheets are not a full frame-by-frame animation atlas unless we ask for one.
