# Starflight equipment

2026-10-20. Original 1254×1254 transparent source is retained as `source.png`.
Reproduce normalized icons and individual missile/drone sprites with
`python art/starflight-arsenal/extract.py`. The extractor reuses the connected-alpha
normalizer, preserves each complete silhouette and strips metadata.

Production brief: nine isolated premium glossy 3D equipment objects in a three-by-three
transparent sheet. Dark titanium and white ceramic hardware with crisp bevels,
readable at small phone sizes. Twin cyan rail-lance barrels, violet hyper cannon,
paired coral homing missiles, emerald weaving missile, paired cyan escort drones,
orange nova bomb, teal cryogenic stasis generator, emerald repair capsule and azure
shield generator. No text, frames, plus/minus symbols, flat pictograms or characters.

The runtime manifest records exact source/runtime hashes, crops, pivots and the
individual projectile orientation. These are equipment illustrations; actual
weapon trajectories and collision remain model-owned.
